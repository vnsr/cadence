# Cadence – weekly planner (offline PWA)

Plan your week in time blocks per area, track what actually happened, and close each week with a short review.

## Install (once)
1. Repo → **Settings → Pages** → branch `main`, folder `/ (root)` → Save.
2. Open **https://vnsr.github.io/cadence/** in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Open it once while online.

## How to use it
- **Areas & targets** (Settings): starts with Applications 12 h, Interview prep 5 h, Learning 4 h, Networking 2 h, Fitness 4 h, Family (no target), Admin & Behörden 2 h, Side projects 3 h. Rename, recolour and change them freely.
- **Day:** tap the grid to add a block (snaps to half hours). Planned blocks are dashed, done blocks solid, skipped ones faded. **Also on** copies a block to other days of the week. **Mark past blocks done** after a day that went to plan.
- **Track now:** tap an area to start a timer; **Stop** saves a done block (rounded to 5 minutes).
- **Week:** hours done and planned per area against the target, the whole week at a glance, **Copy last week**, templates, and **Add to Calendar** for the planned blocks.
- **Review** (the tab gets a dot at the weekend): what went well, what got in the way, energy 1–5, and up to three focus points that then show on every day of next week.
- **Trends:** hours per area per week, averages against targets, and energy over time.
- **Countdown** (Settings): e.g. "47 days to new job" in the header.
- **Export CSV** (Settings → Backup): every block with date, time, area and minutes, for example as a record of job-search activity.

## Sync & backups
Settings → Automatic sync with `username/my-app-data` and your token; Cadence writes only to `cadence/` (blocks in one file per month). Backup to Files and import as in the other apps.
